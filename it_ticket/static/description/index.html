Support HelpDesk System

The ultimate, lightweight ticket management system for your company.

Key Features

  • Role Based Security: Employees can only see and manage their own tickets, while Managers have full access to all tickets.
  • Interactive Kanban Board: Track ticket progress visually from 'New' to 'Closed' with locked dragging for strict workflow adherence.
  • Built-in Chatter: Seamless communication! Add comments, log internal notes, and attach screenshots directly inside the ticket.
  • Auto-Sequencing: Automatically generates professional and unique ticket IDs (e.g., TKT-0001).
  • 100% Free & Open Source: Ready to use for businesses of all sizes without any extra enterprise costs.
